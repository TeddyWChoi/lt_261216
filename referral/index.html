Event Rules & Important Notices

Event Period

  • December 16, 2026 – February 14, 2027. All times are based on Eastern Standard Time (US).

Eligibility

  • New Players: Papaya Play accounts created on or after December 16, 2026.
  • Returning Players: players with no La Tale login history for 60 days or more before the event begins. Returning status remains even after logging in during the event.
  • Players who have joined with a referral code cannot invite other players.
  • Only one invitation code can be entered per eligible account.

Rewards

  • The event entry is completed once the invited player logs in to Papaya Play and La Tale.
  • Inviting players can receive per-invite rewards for up to {{ cfg.maxReferrals }} eligible invitees.
  • Level and milestone rewards are not granted immediately. Eligibility is calculated based on progress through the previous day.
  • All rewards are sent to your in-game Mailbox and must be claimed within 14 days.

Lucky Draw

  • 5 winners in total will be drawn from eligible inviting and invited players after the event ends. Invited players must reach Ascension Lv. {{ cfg.luckyDrawLevel }} to be eligible.
  • No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Winners will be contacted to collect shipping information.

Other

  • Participation using abnormal methods (multiple accounts, the same IP or device, etc.) may result in disqualification and reward removal.
  • Event details and rewards may be changed without prior notice.
