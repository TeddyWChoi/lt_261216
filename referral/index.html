IMPORTANT NOTICES

  • The event runs from December 16, 2026 to February 14, 2027.
  • New Players: Papaya Play accounts created on or after December 16, 2026.
  • Returning Players: players with no La Tale login history for 60 days or more before the event begins. Returning status remains even after logging in during the event.
  • Players who have joined with an invitation code cannot invite other players.
  • Only one invitation code can be entered per eligible account.
  • An invitation is complete once the invited player logs in to La Tale through Papaya Play.
  • Inviting players can receive per-invite rewards for up to {{ cfg.maxReferrals }} eligible invitees.
  • Level and milestone rewards are not granted immediately. Eligibility is calculated based on progress through the previous day.
  • All rewards are sent to your in-game Gift Bundle and must be claimed within 14 days.
  • Lucky Draw: 5 winners in total will be drawn from eligible inviting and invited players after the event ends. Invited players must reach Ascension Lv. {{ cfg.luckyDrawLevel }} to be eligible.
  • No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Winners will be contacted to collect shipping information.
  • Participation through abnormal means (e.g., multiple accounts or a shared IP or device) may result in disqualification and removal of rewards.
  • Event details and rewards are subject to change without prior notice.
  • All times are based on Eastern Standard Time (US).
